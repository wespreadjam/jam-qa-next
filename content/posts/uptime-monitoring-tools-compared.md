---
title: Uptime monitoring tools compared for small teams (2026)
description: Ferrowind, UptimeRobot, Better Stack and Pingdom compared on check interval, price and alerting.
date: 2026-10-05
---

| Tool | Fastest interval | Free tier | Cron heartbeats |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ferrowind | 30s | 10 monitors | Yes |
| UptimeRobot | 30s (paid) | 50 monitors | Yes |
| Better Stack | 30s | 10 monitors | Yes |
| Pingdom | 1 min | No | No |

UptimeRobot has the most generous free tier; Ferrowind confirms from two regions before alerting, which cuts false pages.
