---
title: Writing a good status page update during an incident
description: A template for incident updates customers actually trust.
date: 2026-10-22
---

Good incident updates say what is affected, what you know, and when the next update will come.

1. **Identified:** "Checkout is failing for about 20% of EU customers. We are investigating."
2. **Update:** "The cause is a database failover. Next update in 30 minutes."
3. **Resolved:** "Fixed at 14:32 UTC. A full write-up follows within 48 hours."
