## What each tool tracks

### Jam: six engines, 50 to 1,000 prompts

Jam tracks ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews and Microsoft Copilot on every plan. You choose between 50 and 1,000 tracked prompts and set scans to weekly, every other day or daily ([Jam pricing](https://spreadjam.com/pricing)). Jam's Visibility Score is the share of your tracked prompts where you are cited ([Jam](https://spreadjam.com/)).

Jam does not list Claude or Amazon Rufus among its six engines ([Jam pricing](https://spreadjam.com/pricing)). If those are where your buyers ask questions, that is a gap.

### Bluefish AI: enterprise monitoring, accuracy and commerce

Bluefish describes three suites: AI Monitoring, AI Optimization and AI Measurement. It says it tracks "10+ LLMs daily including Alexa for Shopping, plus 15+ provider integrations" ([Bluefish](https://www.bluefishai.com/)). Its press release names ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Claude, Perplexity and Amazon Rufus, and says the platform processes millions of prompts and responses per day across customers ([PR Newswire, April 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/bluefish-raises-43-million-series-b-to-power-agentic-marketing-for-the-fortune-500-302741124.html)).

Bluefish prompts are simulated, controlled sets configured around audience segments, not a feed of real user prompts ([Dageno](https://www.dageno.ai/blog/bluefish-ai-review)). Profound, a competitor, says Bluefish keeps a static prompt baseline approved with the customer and that its strategy services team handles custom setups ([Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/articles/profound-vs-bluefish)).

Bluefish added AI Accuracy and Brand Vault in May 2026. It extracts factual claims from AI answers, checks them against a verified source of truth and scores errors by severity ([Readyt](https://www.readyt.ai/blog/bluefish-ai-alternatives), [Meev](https://meev.ai/reviews/bluefish-ai)). Jam has no equivalent module.

## Pricing side by side

**Jam** publishes a price. The pricing page builds a plan from a prompt slider with stops at 50, 200, 400, 600, 800 and 1,000 prompts. The default, 400 prompts, costs $299 per month and includes scans every other day across six platforms and 2,000 work credits per month ([Jam pricing](https://spreadjam.com/pricing)). Every plan gets the full product, including competitor gap analysis, Google Search Console integration and the chat agent with 50+ tools.

Work credits pay for what Jam produces: about 35 credits for a researched and written article, 20 more to ship it as a pull request, about 50 for a deep research report and about 10 to 40 for a chat task. 2,000 credits covers about 36 articles written and shipped as pull requests. Top-ups never expire. The trial lasts 14 days, needs a card and charges you only if you do not cancel before it ends ([Jam pricing](https://spreadjam.com/pricing)). Jam sells Enterprise plans for more than 1,000 prompts or custom terms through a booked call, with no published price.

**Bluefish AI** does not publish pricing. The site routes visitors to "Request a demo" ([Bluefish](https://www.bluefishai.com/)). Third-party estimates differ. Meev cites an OpenLens estimate of $150,000 to $500,000+ a year plus $15,000 to $100,000 for implementation ([Meev](https://meev.ai/reviews/bluefish-ai)). CheckThat estimates $100,000 to $500,000+ a year, using tiers it labels illustrative rather than official ([CheckThat](https://checkthat.ai/brands/bluefish-ai/pricing)). Treat both as estimates, not quotes.

## Which tool is better for competitor gap analysis?

For a content marketer, a gap analysis answers one question: which prompts do competitors get cited for that you do not, and what page would change that. Jam includes competitor gap analysis, a citation dashboard, Search Console data, SEO data and content planning on every plan, and its homepage describes finding "where you're not being cited in all AI platforms" ([Jam](https://spreadjam.com/), [Jam pricing](https://spreadjam.com/pricing)). The same plan then writes the article and opens it as a pull request.

Bluefish covers competitive benchmarking inside its monitoring suite and adds Citation Impact, which identifies the content sources with the most influence on AI responses ([Bluefish](https://www.bluefishai.com/), [Dageno](https://www.dageno.ai/blog/bluefish-ai-review)). Execution runs through its optimization suite and a services team ([Bluefish](https://www.bluefishai.com/)). That fits a team with agency and brand departments, and is more than a two-person content team will use.

## Beyond monitoring: what you can do with the result

Jam is built to act on the gaps it finds. Beyond articles, the product includes a shared inbox, lead enrichment, mailbox warm-up and custom automations for outreach, and you can approve drafts and review pull requests from Slack and GitHub ([Jam](https://spreadjam.com/)).

Bluefish extends in other directions: agentic commerce including Amazon Rufus and Alexa for Shopping, paid media, and brand accuracy ([Bluefish](https://www.bluefishai.com/), [Meev](https://meev.ai/reviews/bluefish-ai)).

## Who should pick which

**Pick Jam if:**
- You are a founder, growth engineer or content lead and want to start this week with a trial and a published price.
- Your priority is closing competitor citation gaps with new articles, not a brand governance programme.
- Your buyers ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, AI Overviews or Copilot.
- You want outreach and content distribution in the same product.

**Pick Bluefish AI if:**
- You are a Fortune 500 or similar enterprise. About 10% of the Fortune 500 use it, including Adidas, American Express, Hearst, LVMH and Ulta Beauty ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/bluefish-raises-43-million-series-b-to-power-agentic-marketing-for-the-fortune-500-302741124.html)).
- You need factual accuracy scoring against a verified brand source, or Amazon Rufus and shopping coverage.
- You want a vendor team to run strategy and execution, and can budget six figures. CheckThat reports Bluefish does not compete for mid-market or SMB business ([CheckThat](https://checkthat.ai/brands/bluefish-ai/pricing)), and Meev reports reviewers describing the best fit as 50+ person marketing teams ([Meev](https://meev.ai/reviews/bluefish-ai)).

## FAQ

### Is Jam an alternative to Bluefish AI?
Yes for teams below enterprise scale. Both monitor how AI engines cite your brand. Jam adds a published $299/month starting price, self-serve trial and article shipping; Bluefish adds Claude and Amazon Rufus coverage and brand accuracy scoring.

### How much does Bluefish AI cost?
Bluefish does not publish prices ([Bluefish](https://www.bluefishai.com/)). Third-party estimates run from $100,000 to $500,000+ a year ([CheckThat](https://checkthat.ai/brands/bluefish-ai/pricing), [Meev](https://meev.ai/reviews/bluefish-ai)).

### How much does Jam cost?
The default plan is $299 per month for 400 tracked prompts, scanned every other day across six platforms, with 2,000 work credits ([Jam pricing](https://spreadjam.com/pricing)).

### Which AI engines does each tool track?
Jam: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews and Microsoft Copilot. Bluefish: ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Claude, Perplexity and Amazon Rufus, plus other LLMs it does not name ([Jam pricing](https://spreadjam.com/pricing), [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/bluefish-raises-43-million-series-b-to-power-agentic-marketing-for-the-fortune-500-302741124.html), [Bluefish](https://www.bluefishai.com/)).

### Can I try Bluefish before buying?
Bluefish routes visitors to a demo request ([Bluefish](https://www.bluefishai.com/)). Jam offers a 14-day trial ([Jam pricing](https://spreadjam.com/pricing)).
