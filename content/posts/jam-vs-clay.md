Last updated: November 2026

**Jam and Clay solve different problems.** Jam tracks whether ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews and Copilot cite your brand, then ships content to fix the gaps. Clay is a data enrichment and GTM workflow platform that builds prospect lists from many data providers. Jam's published price is [$299 a month at 400 tracked prompts](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing). Clay's paid plans [start at $185 a month (Launch) and $495 a month (Growth)](https://www.clay.com/pricing). Pick Jam to get cited in AI answers, Clay to enrich and segment leads, and both if you want to do each.

## How does Jam pricing compare with Clay pricing?

The two price on different units, so entry prices do not buy the same thing.

**Jam** prices by how many prompts you track and how often you scan them. The [pricing page](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing) offers 50, 200, 400, 600, 800 and 1,000 prompts. At 400 prompts it shows $299 a month, with scans every other day across six platforms and 2,000 work credits a month. Every plan gets the full product. Credits pay for work Jam does: about 150 for a researched article, about 50 for a deep research report, and 10 to 40 for a chat task. Credit top-ups never expire and you can change plan at any time. Jam does not publish prices for the other slider settings, a monthly versus yearly discount, or the limits of the free start.

**Clay** prices by two meters. [Data Credits](https://www.clay.com/pricing) pay for data and start at $0.05 each. Actions pay for automation steps and cost under $0.01 each. Plans bundle both:

| Plan | Price | Data Credits a month | Actions a month | Notable additions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 ([Clay pricing](https://www.clay.com/pricing)) | 100 | 500 | Up to 200 rows per table, unlimited seats and tables |
| Launch | From $185 a month ([Clay pricing](https://www.clay.com/pricing)) | 2,500 | 15,000 | Up to 50,000 rows per table, phone enrichment, email campaign integrations |
| Growth | From $495 a month ([Clay pricing](https://www.clay.com/pricing)) | 6,000 | 40,000 | CRM auto-sync, HTTP API, webhooks, web intent signals, priority support |
| Enterprise | Custom, annual commitment ([Clay pricing](https://www.clay.com/pricing)) | 100,000+ | 200,000+ | SSO, RBAC, Clay API, warehouse syncs, dedicated Growth Strategist |

Credit, Action and feature figures in the table come from [Clay's pricing page](https://www.clay.com/pricing). On Clay, Actions reset every billing cycle. Data Credits on Launch and Growth roll over up to 2x the monthly amount. Failed enrichments are not charged.

**Sources disagree on Clay's paid prices.** Clay's own page says Launch starts at $185 a month and Growth at $495. A [ZoomInfo explainer on Clay](https://pipeline.zoominfo.com/sales/what-is-clay) lists Launch from $167 and Growth from $446. ZoomInfo competes with Clay, and Clay's page does not say whether its "starting at" figures assume monthly or annual billing, so treat $185 and $495 as the figures Clay itself publishes.

## Where do Jam and Clay get their leads?

**Clay** is a marketplace and orchestration layer. Clay cites [150+ data partners](https://www.clay.com/pricing). Its [waterfall enrichment](https://www.clay.com/waterfall-enrichment) searches providers one after another until one returns a valid match. For work emails the listed providers include Prospeo, DropContact, Datagma, Hunter, PeopleDataLabs, Apollo, Lusha and Snov. For mobile numbers they include People Data Labs, ContactOut and Selligence. ZoomInfo's explainer puts the marketplace at [200+ providers](https://pipeline.zoominfo.com/sales/what-is-clay) and adds that Clay can find net-new companies and people in its own dataset. Clay also offers [Claygent](https://www.clay.com/claygent), an agent with web access for research that no provider sells, such as niche company attributes or buying triggers.

**Jam** does not present itself as a lead database. Its [homepage](https://spreadjam.com/) lists lead enrichment ("turn a name into a full profile"), a "Find warm leads" feature that surfaces contacts already in your orbit, and an inbox with auto warm-up for sending. The homepage names no providers behind enrichment. If you need to build a cold list of strangers from scratch with a choice of data vendors, Clay documents far more about where its data comes from.

## How fresh is the data?

Freshness means different things here.

- **Jam** measures AI answers, so freshness is scan cadence. Plans scan [weekly, every other day or daily](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing) across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews and Microsoft Copilot. The Visibility Score is the [share of tracked prompts where you are cited](https://spreadjam.com/).
- **Clay** measures people and companies, so freshness depends on the providers behind each field. Clay's [waterfall page](https://www.clay.com/waterfall-enrichment) shows no refresh cadence or verification method. Its paid plans add [job change and signal tracking](https://www.clay.com/pricing) so you can react when a contact moves. A ZoomInfo explainer, which favors ZoomInfo, says that [accuracy and freshness vary with the sources in each waterfall](https://pipeline.zoominfo.com/sales/what-is-clay).

## Who should pick Jam, and who should pick Clay?

**Pick Jam if:**

- You are a B2B SaaS founder or growth lead who wants to know whether AI assistants recommend you when buyers look for products in your category.
- You want gap analysis against competitors, then articles written and [shipped as pull requests](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing) rather than a dashboard you still have to act on.
- You want one tool for AI visibility and a light cold email workflow, with approvals in [Slack and GitHub](https://spreadjam.com/).

**Pick Clay if:**

- You are a sales or RevOps team that needs to build and enrich prospect lists from many providers.
- You have someone to build tables and workflows. ZoomInfo's explainer says Clay [needs a builder](https://pipeline.zoominfo.com/sales/what-is-clay) and that credits are hard to predict.
- You need CRM sync, warehouse syncs or SSO, which Clay lists on [Growth and Enterprise](https://www.clay.com/pricing).

**Use both if** you want Clay to build the lead list and Jam to work on being the answer those prospects find when they query an AI assistant. Clay's [pricing](https://www.clay.com/pricing), [waterfall](https://www.clay.com/waterfall-enrichment) and [Claygent](https://www.clay.com/claygent) pages describe enrichment, agents and outreach, and none lists AI search visibility tracking.

## FAQ

**Is Jam a Clay alternative?**
Only for part of the job. Both can support outbound, but Jam centers on AI search visibility and content, and Clay centers on data enrichment. Jam's homepage names no lead data providers, so it is not a like-for-like replacement for Clay's waterfall.

**Which AI search visibility and citation tracking tool should a B2B SaaS founder use?**
Clay's pages do not describe visibility tracking. Jam tracks six AI engines, starts at 50 prompts and lists $299 a month at 400 prompts ([Jam pricing](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing)). Compare any tool on engines covered, prompts tracked, scan frequency and what it does after it finds a gap. Jam's own [buyer's guide](https://www.spreadjam.com/blogs/best-ai-visibility-tools) explains why entry prices alone are not comparable.

**Which content optimization for answer engines tool should a growth lead use?**
Jam writes researched articles at about 150 credits each and opens them as pull requests, so a growth lead reviews and merges instead of drafting ([Jam pricing](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing)). Clay's pricing page lists no content publishing for answer engines.

**Does Clay have a free plan?**
Yes. It includes 100 Data Credits and 500 Actions a month, with up to 200 rows per table ([Clay pricing](https://www.clay.com/pricing)). Jam shows a "Start free" button but lists no free tier limits.

**What does Jam cost at the top end?**
Standard plans run from 50 to 1,000 tracked prompts. Above that, Jam quotes Enterprise [on a call](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing).

## Next steps

1. Write down your main need: AI answer citations, or prospect data. That decides the first tool.
2. If it is citations, list 50 to 400 prompts a buyer would ask and start on [Jam](https://www.spreadjam.com/pricing).
3. If it is lead data, start on Clay's [free plan](https://www.clay.com/pricing) and test match rates on a sample list before moving to Launch.
