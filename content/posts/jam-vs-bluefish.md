## What is the difference between Jam and Bluefish?

Bluefish calls itself [the only end-to-end agentic marketing platform](https://bluefishai.com/). It is organized into three suites: AI Monitoring (understand), AI Optimization (influence) and AI Measurement (prove business impact). It targets large marketing organizations with brand, content, search, PR, social, paid media and retail teams.

Jam is organized around a shorter loop. [Find where you are not cited, publish articles that get you cited, and reach the people already in your orbit](https://spreadjam.com/). The same product runs outreach from one inbox. The scope is smaller, and a single growth engineer or founder can run it without a services engagement.

## Which AI engines do Jam and Bluefish track?

Jam tracks [ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews and Microsoft Copilot on every plan](https://spreadjam.com/pricing). Copilot and Google AI Mode are named explicitly.

Bluefish says it tracks [10+ LLMs daily, including Alexa for Shopping](https://bluefishai.com/), but its homepage does not list them. Its press releases name [ChatGPT, Google AI, Claude, Perplexity and Amazon Rufus](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/bluefish-launches-ai-accuracy-bringing-brand-verification-to-ai-channels-for-the-first-time-302762378.html). An independent profile lists [five engines: ChatGPT Search, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Overviews](https://guptadeepak.com/geo-compass/vendors/bluefish-ai), and treats Amazon Rufus and Alexa for Shopping separately. The public sources do not fully agree on the exact list.

Two real differences follow. Bluefish covers shopping assistants such as Amazon Rufus, which Jam's pricing page does not list. Jam names Copilot and Google AI Mode, which Bluefish's press releases do not mention.

## How much do Jam and Bluefish cost?

Bluefish does not publish prices. An independent profile states that [pricing is not published and that you should expect a sales conversation before you see a number](https://guptadeepak.com/geo-compass/vendors/bluefish-ai). Bluefish's homepage offers [a demo request, not a pricing page](https://bluefishai.com/).

Jam's pricing page says [Start free](https://spreadjam.com/pricing). Plans range from 50 to 1,000 tracked prompts, with scans weekly, every other day or daily, and Enterprise for more than 1,000 prompts or custom terms. Every plan gets the full product, and one credit balance pays for the work Jam does:

|---|---|
| Article, researched and written | [About 150 credits](https://spreadjam.com/pricing) |
| Deep research report | [About 50 credits](https://spreadjam.com/pricing) |
| Chat task | [About 10 to 40 credits](https://spreadjam.com/pricing) |

Jam's page says [2,000 credits covers about 13 articles](https://spreadjam.com/pricing) and that credit top-ups never expire. The page invites you to make your own plan and pay as much or as little as you want, and it shows no fixed dollar price per tier.

## Which platform helps you act on what it finds?

Both products go beyond reporting, in different ways.

**Jam** ships work directly. Plans include [content planning, articles researched, written and shipped as pull requests, deep research reports and a chat agent with 50+ tools](https://spreadjam.com/pricing). Drafts and PRs can be approved [from Slack and GitHub](https://spreadjam.com/). Jam also covers the outreach side: [connect every mailbox, triage replies, enrich leads and warm up mailboxes automatically](https://spreadjam.com/).

**Bluefish** connects insight to enterprise workflows. Its AI Optimization Suite [turns real-time insights into ready-to-run tactics](https://bluefishai.com/), and its [Agentic Campaigns](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/bluefish-launches-ai-accuracy-bringing-brand-verification-to-ai-channels-for-the-first-time-302762378.html) and paid media expansion extend that to larger programs. It also offers [custom audits, strategy and execution from a services team](https://bluefishai.com/), which suits organizations that want a vendor to run parts of the program.

## Which platform checks whether AI describes your brand accurately?

This is Bluefish's clearest strength. In May 2026 it launched [AI Accuracy, which extracts and verifies factual claims made about a brand](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/bluefish-launches-ai-accuracy-bringing-brand-verification-to-ai-channels-for-the-first-time-302762378.html). Brand Vault ingests first-party content as a verified source of truth. Each mismatch is traced to the channel and response, scored by severity, and filterable by product line, topic and audience profile. Regulated industries such as pharmaceuticals, financial services and insurance are the stated use case. The press release gives no accuracy benchmarks for the product.

Jam's pages describe [citation gap finding and a Visibility Score](https://spreadjam.com/) and publish guides on [correcting inaccurate AI brand descriptions](https://spreadjam.com/blog). Teams that need formal claim-by-claim verification across a large brand portfolio should weigh Bluefish here.

## Is Bluefish only for large enterprises?

Bluefish positions itself that way. It describes itself as [the agentic marketing platform for Fortune 500 brands](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/bluefish-raises-43-million-series-b-to-power-agentic-marketing-for-the-fortune-500-302741124.html), and an independent profile notes that [pricing and onboarding are not aimed at mid-market or SMB](https://guptadeepak.com/geo-compass/vendors/bluefish-ai). Its customers include Adidas, American Express, Hearst, LVMH and Ulta Beauty. It has raised [$68M in total](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/bluefish-raises-43-million-series-b-to-power-agentic-marketing-for-the-fortune-500-302741124.html).

Its account counts differ between releases. The April 2026 release says [hundreds of accounts across 12+ verticals](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/bluefish-raises-43-million-series-b-to-power-agentic-marketing-for-the-fortune-500-302741124.html). The May 2026 release says [over 100 enterprise accounts across 15+ verticals](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/bluefish-launches-ai-accuracy-bringing-brand-verification-to-ai-channels-for-the-first-time-302762378.html). Treat both as vendor claims.

Jam suits smaller teams: you can [start free](https://spreadjam.com/pricing), and it has an [Enterprise option](https://spreadjam.com/pricing) when you outgrow 1,000 prompts. Neither Jam's pages nor Bluefish's homepage describe security certifications or SSO, so both need a security review before an enterprise purchase.

## FAQ

**Is Jam a Bluefish alternative?**
For teams below the enterprise tier, yes. Both track brand visibility in AI answers. Jam adds shipped articles and outreach, while Bluefish adds fact verification and managed services.

**Does Bluefish publish pricing?**
No. [Pricing is contact sales](https://guptadeepak.com/geo-compass/vendors/bluefish-ai), and Bluefish's homepage offers a demo request.

**Can Jam track Claude?**
Jam's pricing page lists [ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews and Microsoft Copilot](https://spreadjam.com/pricing). Claude is not on that list. Bluefish's press releases do name [Claude](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/bluefish-launches-ai-accuracy-bringing-brand-verification-to-ai-channels-for-the-first-time-302762378.html).

**How many prompts can Jam track?**
Plans include [50 to 1,000 tracked prompts](https://spreadjam.com/pricing), and Enterprise covers more.

**Does Jam run outreach?**
Yes. Jam includes [a unified inbox, lead enrichment, automatic mailbox warm-up and custom automations](https://spreadjam.com/).
