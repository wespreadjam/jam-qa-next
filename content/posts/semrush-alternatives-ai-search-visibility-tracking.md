## How Do You Evaluate Any AI Visibility Tool Before Committing to a Paid Plan?

Before signing up for a paid plan, use the trial or demo period to answer these questions:

1. **Which AI platforms does it actually query?** Ask for a list of named engines, not just a prompt count.
2. **What is the scan cadence on your plan?** Daily, every other day, and weekly produce very different data freshness.
3. **How many prompts can you track?** Confirm the number on your specific tier, not just the maximum.
4. **Does it show competitor citations on the same prompts?** Gap analysis is only useful if you can see who is winning the prompts you are losing.
5. **Can you act on gaps inside the same tool?** A monitoring-only tool requires you to build a separate publishing and outreach workflow.
6. **Is pricing transparent?** Tools that publish starting prices, prompt limits, and scan cadences openly are easier to budget for and compare.

Jam publishes all of these figures on its pricing page. For tools where this information is not publicly available, request a written breakdown before a trial ends.
