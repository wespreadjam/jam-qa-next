A GEO platform trial lets you see how AI answers mention your brand before you pay.

## What you get

- Tracked prompts across several AI engines
- A first scan of your visibility

## How long setup takes

Plan on about one hour of hands-on work to get from sign-up to a first set of results. Treat these times as a planning budget for your team, not a promise from any vendor. Confirm the real timings in your own trial.

1. **Choose your prompts (about 20 minutes).** Write 10 to 12 questions your buyers would ask an AI engine, mixing unbranded category questions with a few that name your brand.
2. **Add your brand and competitors (about 10 minutes).** Enter your domain, your brand name and the competitors you want to compare against, then pick the AI engines to track.
3. **Run the first scan and review it (about 30 minutes).** Start the scan, then read the raw answers and the cited source URLs before you look at any score.

The scan itself runs in the background, so your time goes into steps 1 and 3. Write down how long each step actually took. That record is useful when you compare platforms.
