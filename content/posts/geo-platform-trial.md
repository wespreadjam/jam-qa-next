A GEO platform trial lets you see how AI answers mention your brand before you pay.

## What you get

- Tracked prompts across several AI engines
- A first scan of your visibility

## How long setup takes

Plan for about 90 minutes of hands-on work before the first scan starts. These times are a planning budget for the person running the trial, not a promise about any one platform.

1. **Write down what the trial must prove (about 20 minutes).** Name the decision you are making, the gates the platform must pass, and the person who can accept each gate.
2. **Prepare a fixed set of questions (about 45 minutes).** Draft around twelve questions that cover category choice, product comparison, implementation and named-brand facts, so every platform you test answers the same set.
3. **Configure the trial and record the setup (about 25 minutes).** Enter the questions, pick the AI engines and language, then note the trial dates, question-set version and collection settings so you can compare results later.

After step 3, the first scan runs on its own schedule. Keep a simple log of how long each step really took, because configuration time is part of what you are comparing between platforms.
